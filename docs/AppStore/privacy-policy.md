# Privacy policy

Social Time Monitor does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- The app stores your settings (chosen apps, targets, switches) in your
  device's own storage. They are never sent anywhere.
- Usage figures are computed and drawn by a sealed Apple system extension
  on your device. They never leave that extension, never reach the app's
  code, and are never sent to any server.
- The app has no account system, no server, no analytics and no advertising.
- Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

If you delete the app, all of its data on the device is deleted with it.

Contact for privacy questions: the developer's App Store listing page.
