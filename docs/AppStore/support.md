# Support page (plain words)

For the app's listing and any support URL. Written 27 November 2026.

## Granting access (first run)

1. Open the app and tap **Allow Screen Time access**.
2. iOS asks to share Screen Time usage — tap Allow. This is the same
   permission that the built-in Screen Time settings use.
3. Pick your apps: tap **Choose apps**, tick the Social category and
   any others (YouTube is a good start), and tap Done.
4. Set your target — 1, 2, 3 or 4 hours a day, or your own number.

## Withdrawing access

Settings › Screen Time › Apps With Screen Time Access › turn the app
off. The app then shows a banner explaining what to do; no data is
kept anywhere else (see below).

## Why history is limited

Screen Time keeps a rolling window of usage (roughly the last month).
The app shows what iOS provides — the Monthly view captions the days
it actually has. There is no way to see further back, by Apple's
design.

## Why icons and names come from iOS

The app never learns which apps you picked. iOS shows it a token — a
sealed reference — and draws the icon and name itself. If an app is
deleted, its row disappears.

## Notifications don't arrive?

Check Settings › Notifications for the app, and that Zone alerts are
on in the app's Target tab. Alerts are approximate by design: they say
you're near the target a bit before the figure is final, so open the
app for the accurate total.
