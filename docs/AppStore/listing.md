## Description (draft)

Social Time Monitor shows how long you really spend on screen in the apps
you choose — TikTok-style scrolling, video, messaging, anything — and
compares it with one target you set.

• Pick your apps once, with Apple's own picker
• See today's total at a glance, with a bar that turns amber as you
  approach your target and red when you pass it
• A target per app if you want one, or one target for everything
• Optional reminders at three-quarters and at your limit
• Everything stays on your phone — no account, no server, no analytics

Social Time Monitor uses Apple's Screen Time system. Your usage figures are
drawn by a sealed Apple extension and never leave your device.
